The NHS values

These six values guide how the NHS works with patients, families, communities and colleagues.

Working together for patients

Put patients first, listen to people and work across teams and services to meet their needs.

Respect and dignity

Treat every person as an individual, value their views and be honest and open.

Commitment to quality of care

Strive for safe, effective care and a positive experience every time.

Compassion

Respond with kindness and humanity, and look for practical ways to give comfort and support.

Improving lives

Improve health, wellbeing and people’s experience of the NHS through everyday excellence and innovation.

Everyone counts

Use resources fairly and make sure that nobody is excluded, discriminated against or left behind.

Based on the NHS values in the NHS Constitution.
